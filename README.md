## 🎀 About Me

Hello, I'm **Shalini**! A final-year **B.Tech CSE '27 student at PSIT Kanpur** who fell for the part of tech that people actually *feel*: the first screen, the tiny interaction, the error message that doesn't panic you.

> 💜 **I design it in Figma. I build it in React. Nothing gets lost in handoff.**

My edge is simple: most designers can't ship, and most engineers don't sweat the pixels. I do both, with a love for **calm, clear, human interfaces** 🌿
