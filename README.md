## ✨ Hey, I'm Shalini

I'm a final-year **B.Tech CSE '27 student at PSIT Kanpur** who fell for the part of tech that people actually *feel*: the first screen, the tiny interaction, the error message that doesn't panic you.

> **I design it in Figma. I build it in React. Nothing gets lost in handoff.**

That's my edge: most designers can't ship, and most engineers don't sweat the pixels. I do both, with a bias toward **calm, clear, human interfaces**.
